# Forum authoring

`data/forum.json` stores persistent threads and comments. `/forum` lists them; `/forum?thread=<id>&comment=<id>#comment-<id>` opens a conversation and focuses a specific reply. The same fictional profiles and relationship histories from `flea-bottom.json` appear here. Do not duplicate users. Member registration, submissions, voting and purchases are future work; the current upvotes and Hasocash are authored fictional activity, not real financial transactions or fake interactive buttons.

## Chapter discussions

Maintain one thread per actual slug in `data/chapters.json`. Titles and chapter links are resolved from that dataset on the server, so title corrections propagate. Do not invent a chapter called Jace the Hand: Chapter III, The Weight of Loyalty, hosts that discussion. Read the chapter passages relevant to each reaction. The original 5–10 ordinary participants were a starting range, not a ceiling on the now-busier Taverns. Use a varied interested cast and allow ongoing branches to grow; longer two-person conversations need not introduce a new person in every reply. Gallery's new 15-comment minimum, approximately 25 average and 50 editorial ceiling measure image/video conversations, not a limit on forum threads or live members.

Each chapter thread's `spoilerThrough` is its own chapter slug. Do not use later discoveries, outcomes or identities even if the profiles know them elsewhere. Theories must stay distinguishable from facts; a character's claim is not automatically the narrator's verdict. Readers may discuss secrets revealed in the chapter, but a cast cameo must not invent a new confession, outcome or revelation. Avoid sexualized discussion of young characters. Keep Maela sparse.

Community discussions have no fixed thread cap. The original four cover ghost-dragon theory, quiet chapters, impossible pairings and council-meeting humour; later additions include snacks, edits, modern-AU jobs, changed opinions, reader questions, The Great Game, dragons and side-character perspectives. They allow current-chapter spoilers and collectively remain secondary to chapter discussions. Do not turn every thread into the same six accounts with swapped nouns. Paragraphs, short replies, sincere observations, trolling and sharp disagreement should coexist. The writers-room critic need not be corrected or converted every time; the middle-ground account has independent taste.

## Account arcs and continuing conversations

Every fictional regular can develop, including quiet accounts, critics, faction loyalists, newcomers and cast social-AU accounts. A view can soften, harden, become more specific or reverse after multiple exchanges; friendships and rivalries can also change. A compliment to a rival does not itself establish a faction switch. No account is required to become nicer, and a new view should not erase the older comments that make its development understandable.

Write the change in the conversation first, then record the trigger, affected IDs and current stage in account `continuity` and the [shared ledger](community-running-jokes.md). Track future steps as scheduled until they publish. A natural arc may stay unresolved or take a different direction after a member replies; reread the live branch before extending it. Apply relationship-history events only after a concrete relationship change is earned, not because two accounts agree once. Keep chapter spoiler boundaries intact when a newer gallery exchange spills into an older thread.

Vhaemys is a frequent account with long fictional screen time, not a rare cameo. Suitable forum participation can include simple questions, a small disagreement, favourite toys and returning to something she liked. Keep her seven-year-old perspective and avoid treating her as an adult faction analyst. All activity remains deliberately authored in finite batches.

The current publication policy is a persisted 24-hour batch, with at least one authored response in every consecutive hourly window and a randomized minute from 1 to 59 (seconds may vary too). Store explicit UTC timestamps once; never reroll on requests, builds or visits. `community-schedule.json.policy` describes the cadence and `currentBatchId` selects its 24 slots. Older slots remain as publication history with their original timestamps. Gallery and forum responses may occupy a window independently; a forum response does not require a gallery comment. Replies must follow their parents. This is a finite authored batch, not an autonomous reply bot; review new public member activity before preparing another batch. Scheduled thread/reply IDs belong to each slot's forumThreadIds/forumCommentIds and their timestamps must match exactly. Future content remains absent from public snapshots until due.

Chapter releases are immediate-first. Publish the chapter discussion thread, its main conversation branches, and most related community threads with an already-due UTC timestamp so they appear with the chapter update. Reserve only a small minority of independent follow-up reactions for the persisted schedule. Never place the whole chapter discussion behind a future timestamp, and never schedule a child before its parent is public.

Gallery release-wave budgets now use proportional guidance rather than the former fixed comment counts. They do not impose the gallery's 50-comment ceiling on forum conversations. Existing hourly slots and already-authored times stay intact when adding activity.

## Replies, moderation and rewards

Comments reference `entryId` (thread ID), independent `authorId`, optional `parentId`, stable ID, body and UTC ISO `publishedAt`. Parents precede descendants in the file and publish no later than descendants. Multiple reply levels are supported; visual indentation stops growing after two levels, but parent links retain the complete conversation. The shared publication boundary withholds future threads, comments and descendants of unpublished parents. Forum comments join gallery comments in notifications and profile counts.

Jace (`cast-jacaelon-targaryen`) is the fictional moderator. A warning has `moderation: {rule, targetCommentId}` and is a reply to the affected comment. Only this account can issue stored moderator warnings. He responds to a specific violation, such as a personal insult, not merely criticism of the story or of himself. Initial rules: argue the take rather than the person; respect the spoiler boundary; no spam or pile-ons. Sharpness, disagreement and silly bait are not all moderation incidents. Do not silently erase an argument to make everybody friends.

`upvoterIds` contains distinct existing accounts other than the author. `awards` contains `{fromUserId, amount}` for positive integer amounts of fictional Hasocash, also never from the recipient. Choose reactions that make sense for those accounts; do not blindly have the target of an insult upvote the insult. The public projection exposes only upvote count and Hasocash total. The hexagonal H medallion is a community award with no wallet, purchase or monetary claim.

Append real release notes to `update-notes.json` when the forum gains a feature; do not invent historical launch dates. Preserve existing comments and IDs. Run `npm.cmd run validate:comments`, `node scripts/test-community-publication.mjs`, and relevant browser/type checks after code changes. `scripts/validate-forum.mjs` checks chapter coverage, participant range, reply ancestry, identities, moderation, rewards and publication timing.
