## Taverns-wide running jokes

These jokes may recur across suitable Taverns discussions rather than belonging to one reel. They should grow through callbacks and participant memory, not appear as a checklist in every thread. Vhaemys is overwhelmingly popular with the players, but admiration does not make her faultless or erase criticism of the adults around her.

- **Vhaemys v. the necklace:** Vhaemys accepted the earrings but never abandoned her claim to Lorenah's necklace. Treat later attempts as appeals, enforcement actions or an unresolved royal case. Her possessiveness can be funny without making the necklace canonically hers.
- **Lucas Enabling Department:** Lucas has a habit of receiving one look from Vhaemys and finding a way to help her make the situation worse. He can be called her accomplice, logistics officer or enabling department when the scene supports it.
- **Kitchen Workers' Union:** Royal crises repeatedly become the kitchen staff's problem through punishments, raids, pies, biscuits and impossible hours. Tavern regulars can track overtime, demand representation or threaten a fictional labour action; do not make abused servants or victims the punchline.
- **Council agenda bureaucracy:** The court turns personal problems into agendas, committees, decrees and paperwork. Regulars can propose unnecessary departments or celebrate the rare meeting that receives an agenda in advance.
- **Evidence Committee and receipts warrants:** Unsupported theories invite the imagined arrival of `receipts_since_2014`, an evidence committee or a warrant for citations. This should police overconfident claims playfully, not shut down all speculation.
- **The court group chat:** The royal court behaves like a badly managed family group chat: nobody reads the same message, someone needs to be muted, Jace keeps screenshots and the family desperately needs read receipts.
- **Gaelor PR Department:** `gaelord_apologist` keeps attempting Gaelor public relations while becoming increasingly exhausted by the material. A failed defence may invite another Jace Headquarters recruitment attempt, but reluctant praise for Jace is not a faction conversion.
- **Alester departmental custody:** `jacejpeg` and `swordwife` can dispute which fandom department has custody of Alester, whether he is on loan and when he must be returned. Their shared appreciation does not erase their different loyalties.
- **Jace's receipt holster:** Jace produces old statements, contradictions and useful information with quick-draw speed. This can cross over with the Evidence Committee without making him omniscient or giving him knowledge he does not have.
- **Brotherhood server outage:** Communication between royal brothers repeatedly fails at the worst moment. Treat the brotherhood as an unreliable service with outages, maintenance notices and no useful support desk.
- **Pocket Cake candidate:** Pocket cake can win an MVP poll, engagement count or mock election and be proposed as the realm's least dangerous ruler. This is the political branch of the pocket-cake joke, not a compulsory response to every Vhaemys appearance.
- **Vhaemys's jurisdiction:** Things Vhaemys claims can acquire mock agencies such as the Pocket Cake Department, Toy Harbour Authority or Necklace Enforcement Office. Keep the institutions imaginary and the voice childlike rather than turning them into canon policy.
- **Toy Harbour Planning Office:** The social-AU toy harbour can attract absurdly serious planning, shipping and zoning discussion. It is pretend play, not a canonical construction project.
- **Nobody coalition:** When every succession option is alarming, “Nobody” can develop a manifesto, coalition and polling momentum. It remains a forum response to the available candidates, not a hidden in-world faction.
- **Cordin never finishes a sentence:** Maester Cordin repeatedly begins an intervention only for Jace, Baelor or somebody else to silence him, cut him off or take over his sentence. Tavern replies can interrupt a Cordin quotation or keep a count of unfinished sentences. A completed sentence triggers the rarer **Cordin Full-Stop Watch**: treat the full stop as a historic achievement, statistical anomaly or season-finale event. The chapter pattern is real; do not rewrite scenes in which he actually finishes speaking.
- **The Great Game is almost ready:** The Great Game is perpetually presented as nearly playable while another feature, rule or fix arrives first. Players can ask whether a newly announced site feature has delayed it again, treat launch estimates as prophecy or insist they will finally play “soon.” Do not invent release dates, implemented mechanics, match results or rankings.
- **Where is Nymos?:** However large the dragon, war or succession discussion becomes, somebody can pull the room back to the unresolved human question: where is Nymos? The repetition works as both a sincere mystery reminder and an increasingly exasperated meme, especially when everyone is distracted by a louder theory. Lorenah has never heard of him, he appeared in Edmyn's place, disappeared alongside Rhaella and is privately called “their man” by Almar; those clues justify the question but do not answer it. Do not invent his location, employer, fate or guilt, and do not use the callback in discussions whose spoiler boundary has not reached these revelations.
- **New character registration:** When GM HRRM introduces a new character, admin/Jace player `luck` reflexively asks for “boy, age, photo” as though the character must complete an intake form. For a newly introduced man, players may begin preparing the form as soon as his name appears: **HRRM introduced another man** is the opening alarm, while “boy, age, photo” is the intake procedure. Use it for genuinely new introductions, with wording variations; it is not a request for private player information.
- **Taylan has not opened the site:** Gaelor player `taylan` can be treated as someone who never checks the site, app or Taverns and will discover accumulated discourse much later. Do not fabricate posts, logins, private messages or reactions from him; the joke is his public absence.
- **We don't talk about Maela:** Maela was formerly a player character but was so blank that the player community collectively behaves as though she does not exist. This is a meta running joke, not a story retcon: Maela remains canon, her scenes and relationships remain intact, and directly relevant discussion can still acknowledge her. Avoid forcing her into unrelated threads merely to repeat the joke.
- **Admin feature side quests / the endless backlog / “work, Reek”:** Admin/Jace player `luck` finishes one tiny, oddly specific site feature and immediately discovers three more ideas while larger work remains. New micro-features may trigger mock patch notes, an expanding backlog, another Great Game delay or GM HRRM's Game of Thrones “work, Reek” reaction. Keep this affectionate and occasional; do not imply fictional private exchanges or use it to deny completed development work.
- **Gaelor Patch Notes:** Each new Gaelor disaster can be described as a software release: `Gaelor v19.0: diplomacy removed, massacre frequency increased`, followed by hotfixes that fail to address the central problem. This is a strong reusable meme and can cross over with the exhausted Gaelor PR Department or Taylan's failure to read the site. Vary the format, anchor every “change” in events the relevant audience has read and never turn victims into patch-note punchlines.
- **HRRM is the incident generator:** Less commonly, GM HRRM can be described as both the person running the session and the source of the ticket everyone must resolve: “GM has opened another incident.” Reserve it for conspicuously engineered complications so it does not become a generic response to every plot event.
- **Vhaemys approval rating:** Less commonly, ordinary character polls can be derailed by Vhaemys winning by an absurd margin or receiving approval beyond the scale. This reflects the players' established adoration of her, but should remain distinct from the Pocket Cake candidacy and should not erase criticism of her conduct or the adults enabling it.
